02 — DESIGN & BRAND SYSTEM
OfferAlert / working brand: SPORLAG
Design direction: Living Market / Evidence Editorial  
Brand status: Working territory — not legally or commercially locked
---
1. BRAND STRATEGY
The brand must not look like another trust site, review platform, coupon app, compliance service, cybersecurity product, generic AI startup or SaaS dashboard.
The desired category territory is:
Living commercial evidence
The strongest strategic idea is:
> The market leaves traces. We make them readable, layer by layer.
The visual identity should begin with an information principle, not a logo.
---
2. CORE VISUAL METAPHOR
LØFTET / VILKÅRENE / HISTORIKKEN
English:
THE FRONT / THE BACK / THE HISTORY
Every offer has:
a visible front — the commercial promise,
a back — the actual conditions,
a history — how it changed over time.
This must shape the entire UX.
Example:
```text
[ FRONT ]
3 måneder
til halv pris
149 kr./md.

      ↓ reveal

[ BACK ]
Herefter 299 kr./md.
Kun nye kunder
6 måneders minimumsperiode

      ↓ history

v01 → v02 → v03 → v04
```
The product should feel like layered commercial documents, not stacked SaaS cards.
---
3. VISUAL FEEL
The product should feel like:
premium Scandinavian editorial design,
a modern data publication,
an evidence archive,
a living market map,
a calm research product.
Descriptors:
calm
spacious
precise
curious
evidence-led
human
modern
editorial
distinctive
trustworthy without looking governmental
Public pages can be more expressive. Account/review screens should be calmer. Internal Review Studio can be denser and professional.
---
4. WHAT MAKES THE DESIGN UNIQUE
The unique visual grammar should come from:
paper layers,
fronts / backs,
version stacks,
source marks,
editorial rules,
document IDs,
evidence highlights,
changed-field diffs,
timestamps,
annotations,
crop / fold / reveal ideas,
asymmetrical editorial composition.
Avoid relying on generic “brand illustrations”. The information itself is the visual identity.

7. COLOR SYSTEM
Keep color tokens centralized and rebrandable.
Recommended working palette:
```css
--canvas: #F2EFE7;
--paper: #FFFDF8;
--ink: #151515;
--muted-ink: #66706D;
--line: #D8D2C7;
--soft-surface: #EAE5DA;
--primary: #174C43;
--primary-strong: #103C35;
--signal-lime: #DDF45B;
--oxide: #B64C36;
--signal-coral: #F25A3C;
--recognition: #2F7A5C;
--attention: #C98718;
```
Use green as evidence/positive clarity, never as “legally approved”.
Use orange/coral/oxide as signals, not as automatic “bad”.
Reserve strong red for actual system errors/destructive actions.
Do not communicate meaning by color alone.

---
10. SPACING / LAYOUT
Whitespace is part of the brand.
Public pages:
large chapter-like section spacing,
wide content areas,
fewer stronger compositions,
asymmetry is allowed,
avoid endless grids of equal cards.
Task pages:
clear hierarchy,
efficient grouping,
more restrained spacing.
Do not fill every empty space. Do not use “bento” just because there is room.
---
11. BORDERS / RADIUS
Avoid “everything is a rounded card”.
Prefer:
flat editorial surfaces,
thin rules,
restrained borders,
sharp or lightly rounded paper blocks,
evidence containers.
Working token direction:
```css
--radius-sm: 4px;
--radius-md: 10px;
--radius-lg: 16px; /* rare */
```
---
12. MOTION PRINCIPLE
> Motion communicates evidence. Motion is not decoration.
Good motion:
claim layer moves aside to reveal conditions,
offer versions stack over time,
changed fields animate as before → after,
clicking a structured field highlights exact evidence,
history opens as editorial layers,
source-to-fact relationships become visible.
Bad motion:
floating decorative cards,
constant parallax,
AI sparkles,
animation on every scroll section,
unnecessary bounces.
Always support `prefers-reduced-motion`.
---
13. TONE OF VOICE
Tone:
calm,
factual,
precise,
curious,
non-accusatory.
Prefer:
> Vi viser
before:
> Vi vurderer
Good examples:
Et tilbud er mere end overskriften.
Se hvad der faktisk gælder.
Vi har observeret tre versioner af dette tilbud.
Ændret siden sidst.
Tydeligt dokumenteret — se kriterier.
Avoid:
Vi afslører svindel.
Denne virksomhed er utroværdig.
Godkendt.
Certificeret.
AI afslører skjulte tricks.

14. HOMEPAGE DIRECTION
Possible hero:
> **Gør ærlige tilbud synlige.**
or:
> **Markedet glemmer. Vi gør ikke.**
Support line:
> Se hvad virksomheder lovede, hvad der faktisk gjaldt, og hvad der ændrede sig.
Primary CTA:
> Udforsk markedet
Secondary CTA:
> Find mine nyhedsbreve
Hero should not contain too many stats, too many badges, multiple competing CTAs, upload flow, Gmail installation or generic “AI platform” copy.
Hero visual: a layered offer specimen with claim/front and conditions/back.

16. COMPANY PROFILE
Do not lead with a single trust score.
Show dimensions such as:
```text
Pris tydeligt kommunikeret       91%
Kampagneperiode tydelig          88%
Pris efter kampagne tydelig      79%
Væsentlige betingelser synlige   83%
Fungerede som beskrevet          94%
```
Also show observed offer count, sample size, recent offers, latest changes, community confirmations and source/newsletter coverage.
CTA:
> Jeg modtager deres nyhedsbrev
---
17. MY NEWSLETTERS
This area should feel calm and personal.
Each row can show company, new campaign count, pending review tasks, latest change and last observed.
Primary actions:

Find flere nyhedsbreve
Foreslå manglende virksomhed

18. COMMUNITY REVIEW
One question at a time.
Example:
> Var det tydeligt, hvad tilbuddet kostede?
Answers:
Ja, tydeligt
Delvist
Nej, ikke tydeligt
Ved ikke
Show progress, relevant claim context, contribution impact and points after completion.
Target: ~20 seconds.
Never use star ratings.

20. MARKET PULSE
This page should feel like a modern editorial report.
Examples:
```text
01 Månedens tydeligste tilbud
02 Største forbedring
03 Krævede mest forklaring
04 Mønster
05 Ændret siden sidst
```
Use editorial hierarchy, strong typography, page-like rhythm, data storytelling and generous whitespace.
Do not make it a KPI dashboard.

22. MOBILE
Do not shrink desktop layouts mechanically. Recompose.
Critical mobile flows:
Home
Offer detail
My newsletters
Review
Reputation / impact
Consumer review should be especially easy one-handed.
Touch targets: minimum ~44px. Inputs: minimum 16px font size on mobile.
---
23. ACCESSIBILITY
Minimum requirements:
AA contrast
visible keyboard focus
native controls where practical
semantic landmarks
accessible dialog behavior
no color-only state meaning
reduced-motion support
keyboard-accessible review flow
readable evidence text
44px mobile touch targets

24. INTERNATIONALIZATION
Primary: `da-DK`
Secondary: `en`
Rules:
no hardcoded user-facing strings,
canonical enums are language-neutral,
preserve original source language,
translations are secondary layers,

Do not implement:
purple/blue AI gradients,
glassmorphism,
generic AI sparkles,
star ratings,
giant shield logo,
repetitive SaaS cards,
excessive pill components,
stock photos of smiling consumers,
“good company / bad company” color coding,
permanent company trust score,
fake legal badge,
crowded 5–6 line hero,
dashboard statistics everywhere,
hidden provenance,
opaque score without methodology.

26. BRAND CONFIGURATION REQUIREMENT
The entire product must remain rebrandable.
Centralize:
```text
brand.name
brand.logo
brand.tagline
brand.colors
brand.urls
brand.emailSender
brand.recognitionName
```
Do not bake `SPORLAG` into domain logic or database schema.
